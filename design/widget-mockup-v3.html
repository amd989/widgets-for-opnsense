OPNsense Widgets, Redesign v3

Real Widget Board sizes, live widget pages with demo data (Traffic and Firewall update every second).

Real sizes

Every size is 302px wide; only the height changes (148, 306 and 464, measured on the board). The board draws its title bar over the top 44px, so the content starts below it. v2 assumed a narrow 168px small and a 364px medium.

Activity moves up

The 24h chart now sits to the right of the device count instead of on its own row. That fills the empty space next to the count and frees a full row of cards in every size.

Carousel, not scrolling

Cards come in pages that exactly fill the space: rows are fitted to the height, then stretched so there is no leftover gap. Page with the arrows, the dots, a drag, or a sideways touchpad swipe. Capped at 5 pages, like the Adaptive Card version.

Per size

Small: count, new and active, chart, one row of two compact cards. Medium: adds vendor, 3 rows of cards, footer with time, pager and refresh. Large: adds reserved count, MAC addresses and 5 rows.

One footer row

Update time, pager and refresh share a single 18px row (medium and large). Small shows only the page dots.

Unchanged from v2

Colors, status stripes (blue new, green active, gray stale, purple reserved), relative times, and light and dark themes.

Traffic

WAN download and upload up top, then a live 60s mirrored chart: download (green) above the line, upload (blue) below, same colors as the card charts. Medium adds the LAN row; large lists every other interface with its own mini chart. Footer shows WAN totals since boot.

Firewall

Live evaluations per second with a 60s chart, then the donut of each rule's share of all evaluations with a legend (hover a row to highlight its slice). Large adds the busiest rules right now. Small fits the live rate inside the donut.

Shared

All three pages use Widgets/Shared/Web/widget.css and widget.js for the theme, the ready and data messages, resizing and demo mode.
